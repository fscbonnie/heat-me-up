Mini buffet

HEAT ME UP

Heat up your mini buffet in 15 mins.

1heating pack for each tray
330ml1 bottle of water for each tray

Water is not included. Please use your own bottled water, or the water we provided if you added it as a top-up with your order.

Some items in your order are served cold or at room temperature. Items that can be heated come packed in two trays. Repeat these steps for each one.

1

Take out the heating tray

Start by removing the empty heating tray from the bottom of the food tray.

2

Add 1 heating pack

Tear open the plastic packaging, then place the heating pack into the empty heating tray.

3

Pour in the water

Pour 1 bottle of water (about 330ml) into the heating tray.

Move on to the next step quickly, before the water starts to heat up!
4

Put the food tray back in

Place the food container back into its heating tray. Make sure the lid is tightly secured.

5

Let it heat

Leave the food to heat for about 10 to 15 minutes.

Caution: the packaging will get hot!

6

Stir and serve

Stir well and it's ready to be served. Enjoy your meal!
